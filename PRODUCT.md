# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/vanilla JS, one HTML file per page. Chosen because the user plans scroll-driven image sequences (frame-by-frame "3D video" on scroll); that is best served by a `<canvas>` plus plain JS (GSAP ScrollTrigger can be added later without a framework).

## Users

Prospective clients looking for a designer: small and mid-size businesses in the industrial and food sectors, founders needing a brand, and studios needing motion or 3D work. They arrive to judge the work, understand what Gabriel offers and roughly what it costs, then contact him on WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Gabriel Luis Horner, designer. Shows design work, tells who he is, lists services with reference prices. Success: a visitor sees the work, trusts it, and opens a WhatsApp conversation.

## Positioning

A full-time designer since January 2023 who also builds games: brand identity grounded in a briefing, plus the in-house ability to extend a brand into motion (After Effects), 3D (print, game assets, product render) and custom websites. One person can deliver the whole identity package.

## Product Principles

1. The work leads; the interface recedes.
2. Every page ends in a direct path to WhatsApp.
3. Honest specifics over claims: real tools, real dates, reference prices labelled as such.
4. Quiet by default; the brand's 10% colour is a detail, never a field.
